Small MULTIPOLYGON Z face fixtures from the District of Columbia Buildings in
3D shapefile, EGID records selected within 5 km of the Washington Monument.
Source file: BldgPly_3D/BldgPly_3D.shp (DC Open Data).

Stored as open XYZ rings, without attributes or unrelated building geometry:
- bowtie: face 289, a crossed vertical quadrilateral.
- touching_hole: face 257789, an interior ring touching its exterior.
- collapsed: face 30621, retraced edges with no remaining polygon area.
- overlapping_edges: face 795482, overlapping roof edges that stall earcut.
- roundoff_sliver: face 141757, near-coincident edges whose repair produces a
  sub-precision sliver alongside the valid roof region.

Face numbers refer to source-order faces in the selected 5 km dataset.
