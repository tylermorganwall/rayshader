road-profile-dc-convergence.rds

Retained profile specification and context from the first 1,000 complete
DC road features in the local DC GeoJSON example, after the 5 km centroid
filter around the Washington Monument. Contains 16 small solve components.
No native external pointers are serialized; the test recompiles the problem.

Component 9 contains Washington Boulevard fragments 158 and 970, with
8 variables and 14 constraints. On the original OSQP configuration it reaches
100,000 iterations with a constraint violation of about 0.002418 m. A constant
absolute-height, zero-grade witness satisfies every bound (residual < 1e-14),
so this is a convergence regression rather than an infeasible physical model.
Updating rho every 25 iterations solves this component in 150 iterations with
unchanged 1e-7 solver tolerances, objective, and engineering bounds.

Captured 2026-10-31. See test-road-profile-parts.R for full solver validation.
